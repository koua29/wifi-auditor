# Wordlists

**No dictionaries are shipped in this repo.** Drop your own `.txt` wordlists here
(or anywhere in `~/Downloads` / `~/Documents` — they are auto-detected).

Recommended for WPA:

- **[weakpass_2_wifi](https://weakpass.com/wordlists/weakpass_2_wifi)** — pre-filtered for WPA (≥ 8 chars).
- **CrackStation** — large, general purpose.
- A French/your-language word list + hashcat **rules** (`Dict + rules` mode) for
  human passwords (e.g. `word` + a digit).

The app also looks for a couple of tiny starter lists here (`weak-starter.txt`,
`french-common.txt`) — create your own if you want quick smoke-tests.

📖 How to capture the handshake on your LilyGO (Bruce)

The LilyGO records the WPA handshake; the Mac cracks it. Requirements: an SD card inserted, and a client (phone/laptop) connected to the target AP in 2.4 GHz (the ESP32 cannot see 5 GHz).

✅ Method A — Targeted (recommended)

Pick one network and capture only that one — no neighbours disturbed.

  1. WiFi → Wifi Atks.
  2. Target Atks — then Bruce scans and lets you pick the target network from the list.
  3. Choose your SSID, then Capture Handshake (or Deauth+Clone+Verif to deauth + capture + verify in one go).

🛰️ Method B — PCAP Sniffer (broad, all APs on a channel)

  1. WiFi → Sniffer.
  2. The PCAP SNIFFER runs. Bottom-left shows the key counter EAPOL / HS; bottom-right the channel — turn the wheel / press Next to lock it on your AP's channel (stop the hopping).
  3. Press a button to open the params. Set Capture Mode → “Only EAPOL/HS”, and here you can toggle Enable/Disable deauth att.
  4. Hit Deauth Now to force connected clients to reconnect right away.

🎯 Watch the HS counter — then retrieve

  1. Ignore the packet count (mostly beacons). Watch HS at the bottom-left. When it reads HS: 1, a complete handshake was saved — you're done.
  2. Retrieve the .pcap — pull the SD card into the Mac, or use the Bruce WebUI to download from /BrucePCAP/handshakes/ (HS_<MAC>_<SSID>.pcap).
  3. Load it below and start the analysis.
Cleaner & kinder to neighbours (Method B): skip deauth and just toggle Wi-Fi off/on on your own phone — only your AP then produces a handshake.
